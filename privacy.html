Crosswind Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Crosswind is a game made by Anas Deree. This policy explains what information the game collects and how it is used. The short version: the game only collects what it needs to run the online leaderboard, and it never sells or shares your information for advertising.

What the game collects

  • An anonymous player ID. When the leaderboard is used, the game signs you in anonymously with Google Firebase. This creates a random ID that is not linked to your name, email, phone number or Apple/Google account.
  • The nickname you choose when you post a score.
  • Your best score and the kills and distance from that run, plus the platform you played on (iPhone/iPad, Android or web) and the time it was posted.
  • Reports. If you report a nickname, the report records the reported entry, its name, your anonymous ID and the time.

Your best score and chosen nickname are also saved on your device so the game remembers them.

What the game does not collect

No real names, email addresses, contacts, location, photos, advertising IDs or tracking data. The game has no ads and no in-app purchases, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

How it is used and who can see it

Your nickname, best score and platform appear on the public leaderboard, which anyone playing the game can see. The information is used only to run the leaderboard and to remove offensive names.

Where it is stored

Leaderboard data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), which acts as a service provider. See Firebase's privacy information.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your leaderboard entry is kept while the leaderboard exists. To have your entry removed, contact the developer with your nickname and score, and it will be deleted. Uninstalling the game removes the data saved on your device.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Nicknames are filtered and can be reported, and you should not use your real name as a nickname.
