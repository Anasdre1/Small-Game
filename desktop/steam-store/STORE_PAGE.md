## About this game

```
[h2]Steer. Shoot. Survive the storm.[/h2]
Crosswind is a fast arcade flyer. Your guns fire automatically, so every decision is where to fly: around the lightning cells, into the path of enemy aircraft, toward the fuel can before the tank runs dry.

[h2]Enemies that get tougher the farther you go[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Raiders[/b] dive in fast, sometimes in pairs
[*][b]Weavers[/b] zigzag across your path and take two hits
[*][b]Gunships[/b] slow down to fight and fire three-shot bursts back at you
[/list]

[h2]Powerups[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Shield[/b] blocks every hit for 6 seconds
[*][b]Spread shot[/b] fires three ways
[*][b]Rapid fire[/b] shreds anything in front of you
[*][b]Repair kits[/b] patch your hull
[*][b]Fuel cans[/b] keep you in the air
[/list]

[h2]Climb the Steam leaderboard[/h2]
Every run scores distance, kills and pickups. Post your best and see where you rank against every pilot on Steam.

Quick runs, instant restarts, no ads, no in-app purchases.
```
